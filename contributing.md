## 🔄 Client-Side Versus Server-Side

The single most misunderstood thing about skin changers, stated plainly.

**The split**

| **Layer** | **Who sees it** | **Persists** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your client | you | until cleared |
| Server validation | nobody, it is a check | no |
| Other players' clients | only if real for them | n/a |

**What that means in practice**

You apply a skin, you see it. That is real and immediate. It is also entirely local.
No other player's client is told anything, because the item does not exist in their
view of your inventory.

**Why this is not a defect**

It cannot be fixed. Making other players see an item would require the server to
accept it, which is the definition of the item being real. Any tool claiming to make
cosmetics visible to others is either misdescribing what it does or doing something
with your account that you do not want.

**What the feature is genuinely good for**

- Seeing how a skin looks before buying it
- Styling a loadout you like
- Recording or screenshotting with a consistent look
- Enjoying your own view of the game

**The honest framing**

This is a preview tool. Used as one, it does exactly what it says. Used as an
ownership tool, it will disappoint — and that disappointment has nothing to do with
the software.
